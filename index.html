Privacy Policy

App: One Bullet: Ricochet Sniper (com.siradj.onebullet)
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the game does with information. The game collects very little.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, upgrades bought with in-game coins, and settings such as volume) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. What we collect

The game has no account and no server of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or files.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use data from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with ads. Depending on your region, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears the first time you open the game so you can choose what to allow. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

Rewarded videos are always optional. Watching one gives bonus coins or an extra bullet.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 12 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 12.

5. Security

The game keeps its data on your device and does not send it to us, so there is no personal data on our side to leak. Ad requests are sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

7. Contact

Questions: siradjkada@gmail.com
